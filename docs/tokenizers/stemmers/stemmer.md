# Stemmer

A stemmer reduces inflected and derived words to their root form. Stemmers are used by the [Word Stemmer](../word-stemmer.md) tokenizer to reduce the tokens in a blob of text to a common form.

## Available stemmers

- [Porter English](english-porter.md)
